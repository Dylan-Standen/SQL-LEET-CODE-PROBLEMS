# 1757. Recyclable and Low Fat Products
[Problem link](https://leetcode.com/problems/recyclable-and-low-fat-products/description/?envType=study-plan-v2&envId=top-sql-50) · Easy · `WHERE`

## Problem
A simple problem where we are asked to select all Product that are both Low Fat and Recyclable. 

## Approach
Let the table be the (multi)set
$$
P \subseteq \mathbb{N} \times \lbrace Y,N \rbrace \times \lbrace Y,N \rbrace , \qquad (i, f, r) = (\text{product\_id}, \text{low\_fats}, \text{recyclable})
$$
then the task is to firstly introduce the selection $\sigma_{f = Y \wedge r = Y}(P) = \lbrace(i,f,r) \in P \, | \, f = Y \wedge r = Y \rbrace$. Since we only want the product_id, we must introduce the projection $\pi_i : P \rightarrow \mathbb{N}$ such that
$$
\pi_i\big(\sigma_{f=Y \,\wedge\, r=Y}(P)\big) = \{\, i \, | \, (i, f, r) \in P,\ f = Y,\ r = Y \,\} \, .
$$


## Solution
```sql
SELECT product_id
FROM Products
WHERE low_fats = 'Y' AND recyclable = 'Y';
```

## Pitfalls
- None, this problem is rather trivial.
